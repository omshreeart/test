Ask a Question
01How can I place an order?+

Choose a product, request a quote, or send us your design through WhatsApp. We will discuss quantity, material, pricing and timing before confirming your order.

02Do you offer custom sizes?+

Yes, many products can be made in custom sizes. Share the required width, height and material to discuss availability.

03How long does printing take?+

Turnaround depends on the product, quantity and finishing. We will confirm the expected timeline when you ask for a quote.

04Can you help prepare my design?+

You can share your artwork or ask about design and file preparation. Any design work and pricing will be discussed before starting.

05What file formats can I send?+

PDF, AI, PSD, CDR, JPG and PNG are useful starting points. The best format depends on the product; a print-ready PDF is often helpful.

06Do you deliver outside Kathmandu?+

Please contact us with your location. Pickup and delivery options depend on order type and destination.

07How much will my order cost?+

Price depends on size, quantity, material, finishing and design requirements. Send your details for a quote instead of relying on estimated online prices.

08Can I visit the studio?+

Yes, Om Shree Art is located in Sukedhara, Kathmandu. Call before visiting to confirm availability.

09Do you make number plates for every vehicle?+

Please send your vehicle type and the required registration format. Any number plate supplied must follow applicable legal specifications and requirements.
