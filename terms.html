01 / POLICY

Orders & Confirmation

Orders are confirmed after we agree on design, materials, size, quantity, price and timing. Changes requested after confirmation may change cost or completion time.

02 / POLICY

Artwork & Proofs

Customers should check all supplied text, numbers, names and artwork. Please review and approve digital proofs where provided before printing. Accurate and legally permitted content is required.

03 / POLICY

Pricing & Payment

Quotes depend on material, design, size and quantity and are confirmed directly with our team. Payment method, deposit and any tax requirements should be agreed on before work begins.

04 / POLICY

Production & Delivery

Turnaround estimates vary by product and order complexity. Pickup or delivery arrangements should be confirmed for each order. Unforeseen delays may occur.

05 / POLICY

Returns & Corrections

Custom printing may have limited returns after approval. If an item appears to have a manufacturing error, please contact us promptly with your order details and photos so we can review it.

06 / POLICY

Design Ownership

Customers should have permission to reproduce all content and logos they submit. Ownership of newly created design files, where applicable, should be agreed in writing.

07 / POLICY
